Studio & collaboration

A practical way to take ideas to launch.

The studio page describes how I approach selected client, product and creative collaborations. It is a working practice, not a claim that every discipline is already a staffed production company.

Capabilities

Product & software

Web and mobile product development, frontend and backend systems, integrations, payments, deployment and technical maintenance.

Game systems

Progression design, simulation logic, game economies, local and cloud saves, rewarded-ad integrations and release preparation.

AI & automation

Practical AI application development, LLM integrations, agents and workflow automation where they add value.

Creative development

Music, sound, writing, concepts and early development for original stories and experiences.

Process
01 · Understand

Clarify the brief

Define the audience, problem, story and outcome before choosing tools or scope.

02 · Shape

Design the experience

Set direction, map the main journeys and plan the architecture or creative treatment.

03 · Build

Make and verify

Develop the work in focused steps, test the important paths and protect stable systems.

04 · Release

Launch and improve

Deploy carefully, listen to feedback and evolve what has been made.
