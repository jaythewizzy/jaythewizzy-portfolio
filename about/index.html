A LITTLE ABOUT ME

More than one title.
One clear direction.

I’m a software builder, game developer, music creator and storyteller. I enjoy taking an idea from an early sketch to something real — shaping the experience, the details and the systems that make it work.

Over time, I want these disciplines to connect as part of a wider independent creative ecosystem: one that can develop digital products, games, music, books and eventually film and television.

I'm interested in thoughtful collaborators, ambitious ideas and work made with care. The aim is not to do everything at once; it is to build each part properly and let the body of work speak.

Dream big. Create more.

03
