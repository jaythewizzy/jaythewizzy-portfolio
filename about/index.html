About

A builder’s mindset. A creator’s range.

I’m Stephen Omojola, known creatively as JaytheWizzy — a software and AI engineer who also builds games, releases music and develops original stories.

Stephen Omojola · JaytheWizzy

I build across disciplines.

I started freelancing in 2019 and have since delivered web applications, iOS and Android apps, games and automation tools for clients across different industries. My work covers the full product lifecycle: understanding the brief, shaping the experience, engineering the system, integrating services and taking the result through deployment and release.

Alongside client work, I develop independent products through WizzyLux Global Concepts. That work spans software platforms, games, music production and distribution, and original writing. Working on my own products has taught me to think beyond delivery: reliability, store reviews, payments, policies, support and the experience people have after launch.

I’m based in Minsk and work remotely. I’m open to remote roles, freelance projects and thoughtful collaborations.

How I work
