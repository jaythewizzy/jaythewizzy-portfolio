# Project Pricing & Investor Enquiries Update

This is a surgical content update to the Contact and Investors pages.

## Contact page
- Separates project and business enquiries from investment and strategic partnership enquiries.
- Explains that project pricing is tailored after requirements, complexity, deliverables and timeline are understood; no fixed prices are published.
- Asks prospective clients to share the project objective, audience, key features/platforms, target launch window and an optional budget range.
- Clarifies that a direct discussion comes before a tailored proposal and that deliverables, pricing, milestones and timeline are agreed before work is committed.
- Links investment and strategic partnership enquiries to the dedicated Investors page.

## Investors page
- Explains that investors can begin by email or use the Contact page's WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Telegram options.
- Sets the expectation that enquiries are reviewed personally and followed up directly where there is potential alignment.
- Explains that further information can be shared as appropriate after an initial discussion.
- Does not publish valuations, investment terms, financial projections, fixed budgets or promised returns.

No pricing, budget amount, valuation, return, investment offer, payment flow, form, automatic acceptance or new contact channel has been added. Existing styles, layout components, email address, WhatsApp lines, social profile links and routes are retained.
