LUXEN Live on Google Play and App Store A movie and TV streaming platform for web, Android and iOS, with trailers, watchlists, a watch player and several ways to unlock access.
  • A complete streaming product built end to end: a Next.js web app, an Expo app for Android and iOS, and one shared Supabase backend. Available on Google Play and the App Store for phones, tablets and iPads, with thousands of users. Google Play availability covers 178 countries.
  • An access model with three routes: rewarded ads unlock timed watch access, VIP subscriptions run through RevenueCat, and admins issue access codes. When time runs out the player pauses with a clear prompt instead of failing.
  • Billing that holds up in practice: a webhook and a server-side sync reconcile store purchases to the right account, including purchases first recorded under anonymous IDs.
  • Built to stay up: cached maintenance and status endpoints, parallelised reads, and per-IP rate limits tuned so search crawlers and real users are not blocked.
  • Released like a product team: typecheck, lint, Expo Doctor and web build before every build, a closed test with about 40 testers to earn Google Play production access, and 27 Android builds since.
  • Careful with users: consent-gated ads and analytics, push reminders with snooze and follow-up limits, rating prompts with cooldowns, and a content layer of guides, a blog and a methodology page tracked in Search Console.
