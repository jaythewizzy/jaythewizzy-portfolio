The working process

Small steps. Clear decisions. Finished work.

Every project needs its own shape, but the principles stay consistent: understand the goal, agree the scope, create a plan, build in useful increments and test before release.

01 · Discover

Understand the idea

Audience, purpose, constraints, success criteria and the real problem to solve.

02 · Define

Shape the direction

Priorities, structure, experience, technical approach and a realistic first milestone.

03 · Create

Build and refine

Make the core experience, review it frequently and keep the important details in view.

04 · Release

Test, launch and improve

Prepare the release, check the real experience and use feedback to guide what comes next.
