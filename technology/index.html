LUXEN

Built a Next.js web application and Expo mobile app sharing a Supabase backend. Shipped on Google Play and the App Store, available across 178 countries, with thousands of users. Designed timed rewarded-ad access, VIP subscriptions and access codes. A webhook and server-side RevenueCat sync reconcile store purchases to the correct account, including purchases first recorded under anonymous IDs. Reliability work includes cached status endpoints, parallelised reads, per-IP rate limits, push reminders, consent-gated ads and analytics, SEO, and 27 Android builds shipped through release checks.

GrowthOS

Customers fund an internal wallet through Paystack, place orders and track them from one account. A provider router handles verification requests and records activations; Supabase provides authentication and the admin area, with support requests and transactional email through Resend. GrowthOS also hosts the public app-ads.txt and privacy pages used by Coinwell.
