A little about me

More than one discipline. One point of view.

I enjoy taking an idea from a loose thought to something people can actually use, play, hear or experience. That has led me into software engineering, mobile products, game systems, music production and long-form storytelling.

On the technology side, I care about the full product: the user experience, the underlying systems, payments, release quality and what happens after launch. With games and media, I care just as much about atmosphere, pacing, progression and the feeling a finished work leaves with its audience.

I'm building through my own projects while staying open to selected collaborations with people and teams who value thoughtful work, clear communication and a willingness to execute.

The bigger ambition: grow an independent creative ecosystem that can develop and own products, original intellectual property and entertainment — with film and television as a future part of that journey.
