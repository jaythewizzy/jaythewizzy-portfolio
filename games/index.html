02 / SIMULATION

LifeLeague

A life-simulation game spanning childhood, career, family and legacy. Its roughly 1,900-line simulation engine covers career mastery, specialisations, rare opportunities, football seasons and contracts, investments, businesses and generational storylines. A server-authoritative wallet ledger, age-up processing, cloud saves with optimistic concurrency, rewarded-ad claims and a premium webhook are backed by 22 SQL migrations and release-check scripts.

What matters in a game

Readable progression

Players should understand what they are working toward and why each milestone matters.

Fair systems

Economies, rewards and ad interactions should be consistent, transparent and difficult to exploit.

Long-term depth

Collections, mastery and evolving challenges give players reasons to return without making the early game confusing.

Reliable delivery

Local saves, server-side validation where needed and release checks protect the player experience.
